Precision Ranking

Tie-Breaking in NEET-UG Karnataka State Merit List 2023

When two or more candidates happen to secure the same score in NEET-UG, ranks are settled by working down this scale — one criterion at a time — until a clear order emerges.

1
Highest Priority

Candidates securing higher marks in the Biology section of the NEET exam will be allotted a higher rank.

2
Second Priority

Candidates securing higher marks in the Chemistry section of the NEET exam will be allotted a higher rank.

3
Third Priority

Candidates with fewer negative marks in the NEET exam will be allotted a higher rank.

4
Final Tie-Breaker

Candidates who are older will be allotted a higher rank.

Cut-off Trends

BDS Cut-off Marks in Karnataka 2023

Every year, lakhs of candidates compete in the NEET-UG exam and in the NEET-UG counselling with the dream of securing a government quota seat. The BDS cut-off varies each year and the BDS Cut-off Marks in Karnataka 2023 will be based on factors such as:

  • Number of candidates making it to the Karnataka NEET Merit List 2023
  • Number of BDS seats available in Karnataka for 2023–24
  • Number of applicants for KEA BDS Counselling 2023–24
  • College and course preferences of applicants
