BDS Government Quota Cutoff in Karnataka 2026

Admission to BDS Government Quota Merit Seats in Karnataka is based on NEET UG 2026 performance and the counselling process conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority. The cutoff varies every year depending on several admission related factors and is different for each category and college.

Factors Affecting the Karnataka BDS Government Quota Cutoff

  • NEET UG 2026 difficulty level
  • Number of candidates qualifying NEET UG
  • Number of BDS Government Quota Merit Seats available
  • Candidate category and reservation
  • College preferences during choice filling
  • Number of counselling rounds conducted by KEA

Note: The Karnataka BDS Government Quota Cutoff 2026 will be available after each round of KEA counselling. Candidates should refer to the official counselling notifications for the latest cutoff details.
